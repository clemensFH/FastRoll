Bridge in

Approve the escrow, then deposit. The balance appears on L2 once a validator posts the batch that credits it, so it is not instant.

Bridge out

Withdrawing is two steps and a wait. First an L2 transaction to the withdraw precompile, which needs the L2 network selected in MetaMask. Then, once the batch containing it is final, a withdraw on L1 that pays out of escrow.
